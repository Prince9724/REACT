Dashboard me daily/weekly/monthly sales, top dishes, employee performance sab dikhta hai. 1. 🧑‍🍳 Employee / Waiter Panel – waiter kya kar sakta hai? Order modify karna – items add/remove kar sakte hain, quantity badha/ghata sakte hain. Order status change – Pending, In Progress, Served, Completed. Apni sales history dekhna – kitne orders liye, kitni sale ki. 2. 👨‍💼 Manager / Admin Panel – manager kya kar sakta hai? Dashboard – ek page par sab kuch dikhta hai: Total employees Total orders Total sales (daily/weekly/monthly) Top-selling dishes Total collection Employee manage karna (CRUD) – naya employee add karo, edit karo, delete karo. (name, role, contact, salary, login password) Employee performance track karna – kisne kitne orders liye, kitni sale ki. Menu manage karna – dish add/edit/delete, price, category, tax rate, availability on/off. 3. 🍛 Order Management – order kaise handle hoga? Order creation – walk-in guest ya existing customer. Items add karna – multiple items, quantity, price, tax auto lagta. Bill real-time calculate – subtotal + tax = total. Discount apply kar sakte hain. Order modify – final checkout se pehle kuch bhi change kar sakte hain (add/remove/quantity badhao). Billing & Invoice – Invoice me restaurant name, customer name, table number, date, time, order ID, items list, total GST, service charge, payment method. Print ya PDF download.
